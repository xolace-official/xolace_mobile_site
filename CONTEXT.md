# Xolace public site: language

The words the public website uses. Product depth lives in `docs/xolace.md`; this file only fixes which terms visitors see and which are retired.

## Terms

**Mirror**
The short reflection Xolace writes back, naming what is underneath what someone said.
_Avoid_: Echo

**The Fire**
The reflecting AI: it listens, understands, remembers, and writes the Mirror. The only layer name used publicly.
_Avoid_: X brain, the AI brain

**Kindling**
The step that builds a person's supports for the day from the sandbox. A support item it can route to is also called a Kindling (plural: Kindlings).

**Sandbox**
The set of supports Xolace built. The AI routes people to support from it, or builds their set from it; it does not invent support.
_Avoid_: "the AI chooses", "the AI picks"

**Named Kindlings**
Sit with this, Vessa, Xolacers, Lantern, Trusted Bridge.

**Xolacers**
Trained volunteer peer listeners, and the chat with them.

**Vent**
Voice release where nothing is kept.

**Xolace Plus**
The single paid plan: monthly $9.99, annual $59.99, 7-day free trial on the annual plan. Taxes may apply by country.

**Hearth**
The daily-return layer. Internal only, not used on the public site.

## Retired or banned

- **Echo**, **Glimpses**: retired names. Glimpses does not exist.
- **"Built and reviewed by us"** and **"clinically reviewed"**: never written anywhere on the site.
